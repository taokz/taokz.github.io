I build medical AI meant to hold up in the clinic, not only on benchmarks. My work has moved from generalist models, starting with BiomedGPT, a vision-language foundation model for diverse biomedical tasks, to agent systems that reason over images and clinical context, such as RadAgents and RadFabric. Alongside building these systems, I study how they fail: our evaluation of GPT-5 as a medical agent examines how performance changes as clinical tasks grow more complex.
