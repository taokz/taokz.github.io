I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Radiation Oncology at Stanford University, supervised by [Prof. Xianjin Dai](https://med.stanford.edu/dailab.html). I received my PhD in Computer Science from Lehigh University in 2026, where I was advised by [Prof. Lichao Sun](https://lichao-sun.github.io/) and developed trustworthy multimodal AI methods that integrate medical images and clinical text, including generalist biomedical foundation models. My research aims to bring AI into real-world clinical workflows and improve patient care, with current projects focused on medical image analysis and multimodal learning tailored to clinical needs.
